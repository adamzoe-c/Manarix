Frequently asked questions

How much does a website cost?

Packages start at €200 for a one-page site and go up to €1,500 for larger shops and booking sites. You get a confirmed price before any work starts.

How long does it take?

The Starter site has a 3-day turnaround. Other packages are agreed by email. Times start once we have your content and brief.

What do I need to give you?

Your logo if you have one, your text, photos and any links you want shown. If you are unsure, I can help you decide what to include.

How does payment work?

A 50% deposit secures your build slot and the balance is due before launch. You can pay by bank transfer, card link or PayPal.

Will my site be first on Google?

No one can promise that. I set up the basics, such as page titles, descriptions and a sitemap, so search engines can understand your site.

What if I want changes after launch?

Each package includes the revision rounds listed. Further changes are quoted separately. Complete includes 3 months of aftercare.
